CITY FLIGHT — SEVEN CITY TOURS (2026-11-01)

The arcade's City Flight selector features Berlin, Paris, London, Rome, Venice,
Bengaluru and Dubai. Each has 25 stops and its own saved progress. Other existing
cities are removed from the main selector, but their maps, direct links and profile
keys remain available through archived-cities.json. No existing save is deleted.

SHARED SCENERY
The Berlin District study is now integrated into the original Berlin City Flight.
Its detailed 39-building Pariser Platz neighbourhood, Brandenburg Gate, cathedral,
TV Tower and Reichstag dome are part of the full Berlin map. The original entry
point, runway, tour and berlin-city-flight.v1 save key remain. Scenic views are
available from its title and pause menus. The separate district preview retains
its own save keys and now loads the same shared assets.

City Flight owns scenery/environment.js, buildings.js, geometry.js, waterfront.js,
streets.js and city-styles.js. Both flight engines use this material and scenery
layer. Facade variation and refined roofs extend across each existing map, with
bounded roof subdivision outside the detailed Berlin showcase. Tall buildings
and the flat-roof cities use roof types appropriate to their silhouettes. Regional
palettes distinguish brick, limestone, terracotta, Venetian plaster, Bengaluru and
Dubai. Existing bespoke landmark shapes are retained, with textured materials and
prefiltered daylight reflections replacing plain plastic-looking surfaces.

Water sits below the terrain and uses layered ripples, subdued glints, soft planar
reflections and regional colour. Mapped shores receive stone quay edges near
buildings or sloping earth banks by lakes and green areas. Islands and courtyard
holes are retained. Structural decks, piers and parapets fill mapped road bridges;
bespoke landmark bridges are excluded to avoid overlapping their architecture.
Natural shoreline and bridge geometry covers the whole packaged map.

Trees have varied crowns, branching trunks, bark, contact shading and gentle wind.
Rome retains cypress silhouettes; Bengaluru and Dubai include palms. Park planting
is clustered and avoids streets and buildings. Mapped streets near the tour receive
up to 28 crossings, 112 parked cars and small bus shelters. Cars, occasional buses
and walking groups follow mapped road and footway paths clear of buildings and
water. Each city has up to 864 moving vehicles and 800 walkers; Venice instead
has up to 64 canal boats and pedestrians, with no cars. Ten instanced mesh pools and
distance culling keep animation bounded, and pausing freezes the activity.
Berlin's existing central activity is retained, with the new paths outside that
core. Movement follows short loops along mapped paths. Traffic is distributed
around all 25 Berlin stops, including Kreuzberg and the approach from Tempelhof.
Bright vehicle roofs, larger bus silhouettes, small walking groups, and boat wakes
make movement readable from the chase camera. Animation uses elapsed real time,
so low frame rates do not slow traffic to the flight simulation's capped clock.
The central Berlin pedestrians remain visible in Performance quality as well.

Daylight uses scenery/daylight.js to keep direct sunlight, the visible sky, HDR
metal reflections and water glints aligned. Neutral sunlight and cooler sky fill
replace the previous yellow-green cast; roof-edge and facade contact shading are
lighter, and reduced haze preserves distant building colour. Shadow filtering is
softened and its moving camera is snapped to the shadow texel grid. Dubai retains
warmer sunlight, stronger atmospheric haze and reduced cloud cover.
Geometry is merged by location and repeated objects are instanced. Distant banks
and bridge groups are culled together with the background map. Quality settings
adjust render size, scenery distance and reflection frequency. Materials and
textures are uploaded before takeoff; route and inspection views are warmed at
320 by 180 behind the loading screen before the normal display is restored.

MAP AND MODEL LIMITS
The improvements cover each packaged map, not the entire municipal/metropolitan
region. Berlin now extends through Karlshorst to Köpenick. Footprints, roads, water and park
outlines come from OpenStreetMap; roof forms, facade details, untagged heights and
planting are authored approximations. This is not photogrammetry. No LEGO assets
or downloaded architectural photographs are used. Textures and meshes are original
procedural artwork and work offline after the page's local assets have loaded.

Open index.html through the arcade web server. The selector offers six shared-engine tours and the original Berlin City Flight.

All selected cities have 25 named sightseeing checkpoints, matching Berlin. Routes connect nearby landmarks, museums, parks, squares and waterfront sights. Added stops retain their mapped architecture and have gold markers and inspection views; only selected monuments get custom models.

Each city is loaded separately: cities.json supplies the curated route and links to Google Maps; data/<city>.bin is gzip-compressed JSON of actual OpenStreetMap footprints, streets, parks and water. No paid API, credentials or third-party runtime requests are needed. Google Maps opens only when its link is selected. The six non-Berlin cities use a shared Three.js engine and start in the air near their first landmark.

Graphics: merged spatial map chunks, instanced scenery, capped pixel count (Balanced 1920x1080, Performance 1280x720, High 2560x1440), reduced reflection/shadow refresh, and full texture/shader/geometry warm-up before controls become active. Only one city lives in GPU memory. Pause > Graphics changes quality. Each city has its own local progress key, world-flight.<city>.v1; Berlin retains its existing keys.

Map and landmark limitations: geography is mapped, but terrain is flat. Untagged building heights are estimated; facades and landmark silhouettes are stylized procedural models rather than photogrammetry. Catalog model dimensions are approximate. Landmarks are deliberately enlarged for readability (bridge spans stay aligned to the mapped banks), with lower nearby background buildings and gold markers. Rome and Venice have distinct facades, pitched roofs and local details. Route clusters exclude distant attractions and airports. Ring positions are offset from landmarks and raised above nearby collision volumes.

REBUILD
The scripts/maps/build-flight-city.py importer requires Python osmium and shapely. Clip the city source PBF to cities.json bounds with osmium extract (smart strategy, multipolygon relations), then run:
  python scripts/maps/build-flight-city.py CITY-clip.osm.pbf CITY-map.json CITY-config.json
scripts/maps/package-flight-maps.py merges the official OSM land/coastline polygons with the mapped water and writes compressed files. It requires pyshp and shapely, the generated city-map.json files and land-polygons-split-4326/land_polygons.shp. See its command-line help.

LANDMARK LOOK

SMOOTHER FLIGHT (2026-10-30)
Both engines interpolate their fixed-step simulation for display. The visible aircraft eases vertically after collision recovery; camera following and aim use elapsed-time smoothing. The route ribbon follows the displayed aircraft. Cached shadows keep their light projection until refreshed, unchanged guidance retains its DOM, and periodic saves run every three seconds (checkpoints, pause and exit still save immediately).
Run tests/browser/flight-smoothness.mjs with hardware-enabled Chromium to check roof-contact visual jumps, camera easing, unchanged guidance nodes, all 25 hoops, pause saving and steering frame timing in Venice, Bengaluru and Berlin. Test timings are samples, not a guarantee under every desktop workload.

ORDINARY-FLIGHT CLEARANCE (2026-10-30)
Roof clearance now samples the existing obstacle grid ahead of the aircraft and gradually climbs with bounded vertical acceleration, replacing instant height corrections and speed cuts. Analog steering is proportional to stick movement. Automatic landmark camera pulls are limited to the title view; the explicit landmark inspection remains available. Route guidance reuses curve/vector objects. Bengaluru Balanced rendering is capped at 1600x900 pixels to leave GPU headroom on the integrated Radeon; High and Performance remain selectable. These physics/control changes also apply to Berlin.
Run tests/browser/bengaluru-flight.mjs for two 30-second ordinary-flight samples, including low-altitude clearance and continuous steering. It checks altitude discontinuities and abrupt speed drops; the shared smoothness test also checks synthetic building contact in Venice, Bengaluru and Berlin.

APPROVED PALETTE AND BERLIN EXTENSION (2026-11-02)
The user approved the bright colour palette, rounded geometry, lively streets and
transit, and explicitly authorised publication, the extension through Köpenick,
and the same visual improvements in all six other City Flight cities.
Future changes in visual direction still require an actual game render for review.
Reference photographs and the local design image are review material only.

All seven cities use clean pastel materials, neutral white sunlight, rounded tree
crowns, blue/teal water with smooth waves and reflections, and neutral UI whites.
Regional facades, roof profiles, palms and cypresses remain distinct. Only Berlin
uses the six-colour autumn mix; other cities retain green seasonal foliage.
CityStyles defines fixed geographic afternoon solar presets for each city. These
are scenic conditions, not live weather. Berlin keeps 24 October 2026, 13:05 UTC,
19.77 degrees elevation and 215.22 degrees azimuth. East is +X; north is -Z.
Reference: https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/solareqns.PDF
Sky, HDR reflections, shadows and water highlights use the same sun direction.
The central Berlin sky-visibility atlas still uses 96 x 96 x 7 probes and fades at
its boundary. No claim of full global illumination is made.

Park groves cover whole mapped polygons, with buildings, roads, water, sports
pitches, courtyard holes and the former airfield excluded. Berlin has a 24,000
park-tree budget and 1,500 avenue trees; other cities use at most 12,000 / 700
(Dubai 5,000 / 700; Venice at most 100 avenue trees). Planting beds, occasional
flowers, lamps and road markings use spatial mesh batches. Tree wind phases vary.

Berlin has green S-Bahn scenic shuttles and yellow buses/U-Bahn, following the
user's explicit colour preference. Oberbaum's U-Bahn uses its authored upper deck.
Other cities use regional train colours on existing mapped surface rails where
available. No underground trains are drawn above ground. Venice retains walking
and boat traffic, with no road vehicles. Vehicle and carriage pools are fixed;
animation follows elapsed render time and freezes during pause. Scenic shuttle
routes are not a complete railway network, live operation or real timetable.
